Full Sun • Water

The Fountain Gardens

Inspired by Monet's Giverny and Debussy's Impressionism. Sweeping primary fields of red, yellow, and blue encircle a central baptismal font feature.

Palette: Primary Fields
Deep Oak Shade

Suzie's Gazebo Gardens

Modernizing traditional church shade beds under Live Oaks. Wide Cast Iron blades, pickle-like Foxtail fern textures, 'Black Scallop' Ajuga, and 'Funky Flow' Mahonia.

Palette: Rich Greens
Full Sun • Dual View

The Meadow & Arboretum

Restoration of compacted builder clay. Serves two audiences: a serene grotto canopy for visitors and vivid warm/purple sweeps for commuters heading home.

Soil: Clay Remediation
Neuroscience Path

The Labyrinth Gardens

A 4,000-year unicursal sanctuary practice. Resets brain hemispheres through rhythmic walking and color engagement, culminating at a tea table under the pergola.

Palette: Calming Tones
1904 Historic Site

The Chapel Gardens

Honoring 1904 Bexar County founders, ranchers, and dairymen. Designed as a sanctuary reflecting Hill Country foothill vegetation and humble country homes.

Theme: Heritage Reflection
Extension Research

The Gardening School Beds

Living classrooms bridging university extension science with hands-on practice. Raised plots, soil building, and propagation for all ages.

Focus: Active Education
