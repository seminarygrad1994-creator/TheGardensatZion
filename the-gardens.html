Core Curriculum Modules

01

Soil Microbiome & Liquid Medina Protocols

Learn how to activate fungal networks using Medina Hasten, Liquid Humate, and HastaGro in heavy clay and shade environments.

02

Shade Microclimate Design

Master plant layering using groundcovers (Black Scallop Ajuga), architectural ferns, and Cast Iron accent groupings.

03

Seasonal Pruning & Propagation

Practical pruning techniques for Mahonia, Liriope management, and perennial border maintenance.
